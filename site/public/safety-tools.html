Our Built-in Safety Tools

You are always in the driver's seat. Explore the suite of automated and self-service protective features engineered into SecretMsg.

flag

1-Tap Message Reporting

Every message received in your inbox features a prominent report action. Submitting immediately removes the message from your view and queues the submission hash for moderation.

verified_user

Cloudflare Turnstile Bot Shield

Non-intrusive challenge verifying that every sender is an authentic human. Blocks automated spambots, script floods, and rapid-fire trolling without annoying CAPTCHAs.

visibility_off

Double-Blind Anonymous Replies

Recipients can reply to anonymous senders through one-time cryptographic tokens without either party's email or identity ever being disclosed.

filter_alt

Toxicity & Keyword Guardrails

Stateless edge heuristics filter out phone numbers, addresses, sexual solicitation, and malicious terms before they ever reach your inbox view.

cleaning_services

1-Tap Inbox Wipeout

Clean slate whenever you want. With a single tap in Settings, you can scrub all received messages in your inbox instantly with zero recoverable residual data.

delete_forever

Instant Account Erasure

Total self-service deletion. Deleting your account instantly deletes your username, email, active links, and all stored communications forever via SQL cascade.
