Our Safety & Mental Health Resources

If you or someone you know is going through a tough time, experiencing cyberbullying, or having thoughts of self-harm, please reach out to these free, trusted lifelines.

United States & Canada

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

24/7 free and confidential emotional support across the United States and Canada.

Dial or Text: 988

Crisis Text Line

Connect with a trained crisis counselor via SMS anytime.

Text HOME to 741741
