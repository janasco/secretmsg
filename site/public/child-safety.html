Child Safety & Protection Policy

SecretMsg takes child safety with the utmost seriousness. We maintain an uncompromising, zero-tolerance stance against any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM), minor exploitation, or predatory behavior.

emergency Mandatory Law Enforcement & NCMEC Escalation

Any attempt to transmit, solicit, promote, or store Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or engage in the exploitation of minors will result in immediate account termination, perpetual network bans, and immediate mandatory reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant international law enforcement agencies.

01 Minimum Age Requirement (13+)

SecretMsg is strictly restricted to individuals aged 13 or older (or the minimum legal age of digital consent in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information or permit accounts for children under 13. If we discover an account registered by an underage minor, the account and all associated inbox data are permanently deleted immediately.

02 Proactive Automated Safeguards

Keyword & Pattern Quarantine Automated safety filters scan for predatory language, phone numbers, external meeting requests, and flagged grooming phrases.
Immediate In-App Reporting Every message card contains an instant 1-tap report button that flags the message for priority administrative quarantine.

03 Guidance for Parents and Guardians

Parents are encouraged to speak openly with their teens about online communication boundaries:

  • Remind your teen never to share their home address, school name, phone number, or geolocation in public prompts.
  • Encourage them to report or delete uncomfortable messages immediately using the in-app controls.
  • Teens have 1-tap self-service power to wipe their entire inbox or delete their account permanently at any moment.
