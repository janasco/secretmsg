Our Guide to Online Safety

Actionable advice to help you enjoy candid communication while maintaining strict personal boundaries, digital peace of mind, and mental well-being.

1
Never Share Private Geolocation or Identifiers

When posting a prompt on your Instagram Story or TikTok, never mention your current real-time location, daily routine, school address, or home street. Keep public prompts fun and personality-focused.

2
Remember: Feedback Doesn't Define Your Worth

Anonymous responses reflect the sender's current headspace, not who you are. If you ever receive a negative or uncomfortable message, don't let it linger—delete it instantly.

3
Don't Engage with Trolls or Bait

Replying to hostile messages or posting screenshots of mean comments gives trolls the exact attention they crave. Hit "Report", delete the message, and starve them of an audience.

4
Spot-Report Red Flag Behavior

If anyone sends violent threats, sexually explicit harassment, or encourages self-harm, report it immediately. Our safety team logs the incident and takes immediate action.

5
Take Digital Detox Breaks

You don't need to keep your link active 24/7. When school, work, or life gets hectic, pause your link or delete your account. You always have full autonomy.
