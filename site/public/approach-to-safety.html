Our Approach to Safety

True online safety cannot be a cosmetic checklist added after the fact. At SecretMsg, safety is treated as a fundamental architectural pillar embedded into every line of code.

shield

Defense in Depth

Multi-layered security: Cloudflare edge bot protection, rate-limit firewalls, pattern heuristics, and in-app moderation controls.

lock_reset

Zero-Retention Principles

By refusing to store sender identities or behavioral profiles, we eliminate data honeypots that could ever be compromised or leaked.

Recipient Sovereignty

Swift Takedowns

Reports are handled with extreme urgency. Abusive message hashes and IP ranges are quarantined across the edge instantly.

01 Why Anonymity Requires Strict Guardrails

Anonymity can empower people to share heartwarming compliments, candid truths, and romantic sparks that they would otherwise feel too shy to express in person. However, without safeguards, anonymity can also be abused by bad actors. Our philosophy is to preserve the magic of candid honesty while building ironclad shields against harassment, stalking, and spam.

02 Automated Edge Moderation

Before any anonymous message reaches a recipient's inbox, our stateless edge worker passes it through Turnstile bot verification and toxicity heuristics. Messages containing known harassment patterns, threats, or prohibited terminology are immediately intercepted and quarantined.

03 Continuous Evolution

As online trends and slang evolve, we continually update our moderation filters, listening directly to user feedback and working closely with open-source trust & safety standards.
