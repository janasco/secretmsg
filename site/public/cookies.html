Cookies & Local Storage Policy

SecretMsg is engineered without invasive trackers. We believe your online activity across other websites is none of our business.

verified No Cookie Banners Needed — Here's Why

Under the EU ePrivacy Directive and GDPR guidelines, websites that utilize only strictly necessary, non-tracking cookies or local storage do not require intrusive, annoying consent banners. We only store the absolute minimum data required to keep the site functioning and defend against bots.

01 What We Store on Your Device

1. Theme Preference (secretmsg_theme) LocalStorage

Stores whether you selected Dark Mode, Light Mode, or System Mode so the application renders in your preferred aesthetic without flickering.

2. Human Verification Security Token Essential Security

Temporary security token used to verify that you are a real person and not an automated spam bot. Does not track your activity across websites.

3. Authenticated Session Token (secretmsg_auth) Secure HttpOnly Cookie

Only present if you register an account to view your anonymous inbox. Kept encrypted and discarded upon logout or account deletion.

02 What We NEVER Store or Use

🚫 Zero Advertising Cookies: We do not partner with Google Ads, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or ad exchange retargeting networks.

🚫 Zero Cross-Site Tracking: We never fingerprint your hardware or monitor your browsing behavior outside of secretmsg.net.

🚫 Zero Commercial Analytics: No third-party data broker receives session replay recordings or heatmaps of your clicks.

03 Managing Your Browser Storage

Because we only use strictly necessary data, blocking LocalStorage or cookies in your browser settings may prevent you from logging into your inbox, saving your theme, or passing bot verification. You can clear your cookies or local storage at any time via your browser's Developer Tools or Privacy Settings.
