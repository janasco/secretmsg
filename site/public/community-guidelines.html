Our Community Guidelines

SecretMsg is a space for playful honesty, heartwarming compliments, and fun social games. Here is what is encouraged and what is strictly banned.

check_circle What We Celebrate & Encourage
  • ✓ Candid Compliments: Sharing authentic praise, admiring someone's vibe, or cheering on their achievements.
  • ✓ Wholesome Confessions: Secret crushes, sweet memories, and romantic sparks sent respectfully.
  • ✓ Creative Curiosity: Fun hypothetical questions, music recommendations, and playful banter.
  • ✓ Constructive Feedback: Genuine, well-intentioned advice asked for by the recipient.
cancel What Is Strictly Prohibited
  • ✗ Hate Speech & Slurs: Any derogatory remarks attacking race, ethnicity, sexual orientation, or gender.
  • ✗ Harassment & Bullying: Name-calling, persistent insults, body-shaming, or targeting someone's vulnerabilities.
  • ✗ Threats & Self-Harm: Encouraging self-harm, physical violence, suicide, or extortion.
  • ✗ Doxing & Leaks: Posting private phone numbers, home addresses, or intimate photographs.

How We Enforce These Rules

Violations are met with prompt action: automated quarantine, permanent IP blocks, link bans, and, where mandated by law or involving minor safety, referral to legal authorities. We do not tolerate individuals who weaponize anonymity to cause harm.
