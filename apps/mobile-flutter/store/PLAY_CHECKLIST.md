# Google Play release checklist

The canonical Play submission checklist and release runbook is [`SUBMISSION.md`](SUBMISSION.md). Use that document for all setup, submission, and release steps; do not maintain status here.

Two things to know before you start, because both are legal declarations rather
than formatting details:

- SecretMsg is now **ad-supported**. `SUBMISSION.md` carries the Ads
  declaration ("contains ads: yes"), the AdMob account and `ads.txt` setup, the
  consent configuration, and the single `remove_ads` product. The
  no-ads wording that used to appear here and in the listing copy is gone.
- The Play Data safety answers are not in `SUBMISSION.md`. They live in the
  controlled annex [`DATA_SAFETY.md`](DATA_SAFETY.md), which now includes
  Google AdMob, the advertising identifier, ad interaction data, consent and
  regional opt-out, and the children/age-treatment wording. Do not answer the
  Play form from memory or from this file.

Screenshot policy for the banner and the rewarded prompt is in
[`SCREENSHOTS.md`](SCREENSHOTS.md).
