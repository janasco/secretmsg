psychology Who are we?

We’re an independent group of designers, engineers, and digital minimalists with a passion for human-centered technology.

We built SecretMsg out of a shared frustration: modern social media has become too performative, heavily staged, and dominated by algorithms. When everything you post is tied to a public profile and a vanity count, it’s hard to just ask what you're genuinely wondering or say something honest.

We wanted to build something different: a simple, fun, and respectful platform where real-world friends can deepen their bonds through candid Q&A games, spontaneous prompts, and authentic feedback.

Our Philosophy

Our Goal

We believe online friendships should be an extension of the real world, not a shallow replacement. Your online interactions with friends should strengthen your connections rather than strain them.

It’s tough to be completely authentic online today. Everyone feels the pressure to look perfect. SecretMsg removes that friction by letting people ask fun questions, give honest praise, and start meaningful threads in a safe and engaging space.
